## License type

**Standard license** is sufficient — it covers web use on your own site.
You do not need Enhanced unless you plan print runs over 500k copies or merchandise.

## Notes

- **Bulk discount:** 16 images means a 10-pack won't cover it. A 25-image on-demand
  pack or a monthly subscription tier is usually cheaper per image than two small packs.
  Verify current pricing at checkout — Shutterstock changes plans often.
- **Same contributor, multiple images:** Oksana Kuzmina (3) and Max4e Photo (2) —
  no bundle discount for this, just noted for tracking.
- **Download size:** licensed originals come much larger than the 1500px comps.
  Downscale to ~1500px wide before committing, or page weight will jump significantly.
- **Keep the receipts.** Save the license PDFs — they are the proof of entitlement
  if the images are ever queried.
